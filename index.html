about me

a little about me

I'm Sanah, a computer science student at The University of Texas at Dallas and an incoming junior, with a minor in neuroscience. While I don't plan on pursuing neuroscience professionally, I've always been fascinated by the brain and the way it processes information. In a lot of ways, that's what drew me to computer science too. Even though computers and brains work very differently, I love that both are incredibly complex systems built around solving problems, recognizing patterns, and making sense of information.

I've always been someone who learns by building. Most of the projects you'll find here started with a simple question, "I wonder if I can make that?" Whether it's a web app, a new feature, or just an idea I wanted to explore, I enjoy the challenge of turning curiosity into something real. Every project teaches me something new, and that's what keeps me coming back.

Outside of coding, I'm usually watching Formula 1, playing basketball, listening to music, experimenting with new recipes, reading, or going on drives with no destination in mind. I also enjoy filming and editing vlogs because it's another creative outlet that lets me tell stories and capture moments that would otherwise go unnoticed.

I'm always looking for ways to grow as a developer and as a person. Whether that's through internships, personal projects, or learning a new technology, I enjoy stepping outside my comfort zone and seeing where curiosity takes me.
